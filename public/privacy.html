Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Meal plan keeps your plan on your own device. There’s no account, no tracking and no ads, and nothing about you is sent to the person who runs it.

Who runs it

Meal plan is a free hobby project run by Sail, a private individual in Poland. For anything about your data, email mealprep.sail@proton.me.

What stays on your device

Everything you put into the app stays in your browser’s storage on that device: your profile (sex, age, height, weight, and pregnancy or breastfeeding if you add it), your weight log, foods, recipes, recipe photos, plans and settings. It isn’t copied anywhere else, and nobody else can see it.

Backups are files you save yourself, and they go wherever you put them. If you clear your browser’s data for this site, your plan is deleted with it.

What leaves your device, and why

  • Opening the app. The app’s files are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address when your browser asks for them, and may keep it for a short time for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Searching for packaged foods and scanning barcodes. The words you search for, or the barcode, go to the app’s relay on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.), which asks Open Food Facts and sends the answer back. Open Food Facts sees the relay, not you. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver the answer; see the Cloudflare privacy policy. The relay keeps recent answers for a while so repeat searches are quicker, but nothing about who asked.
  • Importing a recipe from a link. The link you paste goes to the same relay, which reads that page and its photo for you. The recipe site sees the relay, not you.
  • Sharing a recipe. The recipe travels inside the link itself. It’s only seen by the people you send it to and the apps you send it through. Opening the link doesn’t send the recipe to any server.
  • Sending feedback. The feedback button opens a public post on GitHub, under your GitHub account. Your app version, device and screen size are filled in for you, and you can delete them before posting.

Searching basic foods, planning, and everything else work without sending anything. The app uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts.

The legal part

The relay handles your IP address and what you searched for only to answer the request you made. That rests on a legitimate interest in the app working (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). Your profile and weight are health-related, which is why they never leave your device.

You have the right to ask what data about you is held, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the app holds nothing about you outside your device, you can do all of that yourself, in the app or by clearing its data. If you think something is wrong, email the address above. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Poland that’s the UODO.

Age

Meal plan is for adults, 18 and over.
