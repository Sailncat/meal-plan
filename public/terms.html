Meal plan is free, it’s a hobby project, and it’s still in beta. By using it you agree to these terms.

Not medical advice

Meal plan is not a medical device. It doesn’t diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Its targets are general estimates worked out from standard formulas and published reference values, not advice for you in particular.

If you have a health condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under or over a healthy weight, or want to change your weight quickly, talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before following any plan.

Food data can be wrong

Nutrition values come from Open Food Facts, which anyone can edit, from USDA FoodData Central, and from what you type in. They can be incomplete or wrong. Check labels when it matters, such as for allergies.

Your data is yours to keep safe

Your plan lives only on your device (see the privacy page). If the device is lost or its browser data is cleared, the plan is gone. Save backup files if you don’t want to lose it.

Use the app for planning your own meals. Don’t try to break it, overload the food search, or use its relay for anything other than the app.
